# Voice Command Guide for Cashbook

To get the best results from the Smart AI extraction, please follow these phrasing styles.

## 1. Simple Expenses (English)
*   "Spent [Amount] on [Category]"
    *   *Example: "Spent 500 on fuel"*
*   "The expense of [Amount] on [Category]"
    *   *Example: "The expense of 200 on lunch"*

## 2. Simple Expenses (Hindi/Urdu)
*   "[Category] per [Amount] kharch kiye"
    *   *Example: "fuel per 500 kharch kiye"*
*   "[Category] per [Amount] ka kharcha hua"
    *   *Example: "dinner per 1000 ka kharcha hua"*

## 3. Linked Transactions (Gave Money)
*   "I gave [Amount] to [Name] for [Category]"
    *   *Example: "I gave 1000 to Ashfaq for dinner"*
*   "I give [Amount] to [Name] for [Category]"
    *   *Example: "I give 1000 to Ashfaq for dinner"*
*   "[Amount] was given to [Name] for [Category]"
    *   *Example: "1000 was given to Ashfaq for dinner"*
*   "I bought [Category] for [Name] of Rs [Amount]"
    *   *Example: "I bought dinner for Ashfaq of Rs 1000"*
*   "[Name] ko [Amount] [Category] ke liye diye"
    *   *Example: "Ashfaq ko 500 dinner ke liye diye"*
*   "[Name] ko [Category] ke liye [Amount] ka udhaar diya"
    *   *Example: "Ashfaq ko dinner ke liye 1000 ka udhaar diya"*

## 4. Income / Received Money (RECEIVED_FROM)
*   "I received [Amount] from [Name]" / "Receive [Amount] from [Name]"
    *   *Example: "Received 2000 from Kashif" or "receive Rs 2000 from Kashif"*
*   "[Name] has given [Amount]"
    *   *Example: "Kashif has given 5000 rupees"*
*   "[Name] ne [Amount] diye" / "[Name] ki taraf se [Amount] udhar diye"
    *   *Example: "Kashif Ne ₹500 Udhar Diye" (Kashif gave = income to you)*
*   "[Name] se [Amount] mile" / "[Name] ki taraf se [Amount] mile"
    *   *Example: "Kashif se 1000 mile" or "Kashif ki taraf se ₹500 Udhar mile"*
*   "[Name] ki taraf se [Amount] aaye"
    *   *Example: "Ashfaq ki taraf se 1500 aaye"*
*   "I borrow [Amount] from [Name]" / "Maine [Name] se [Amount] udhar liye"
    *   *Example: "I borrow 500 rupees from Kashif" or "Maine Kashif se ₹50 Udhar Liye"*

## 5. Someone Else Paid for Me (PAID_BY)
*   "Someone paid [Amount] for me"
    *   *Example: "Someone paid 500 for me"*
*   "[Name] purchased [Category] for me"
    *   *Example: "Ashfaq purchased dinner for me"*
*   "[Name] gave [Amount] for [Category]"
    *   *Example: "Ashfaq gave 500 for dinner"*
*   "[Name] ne [Category] ke liye [Amount] diye"
    *   *Example: "Ashfaq ne dinner ke liye ₹500 Diye"*
*   "[Name] Ne Mere [Category] ke [Amount] Diye"
    *   *Example: "Ashfaq Ne Mere dinner ke ₹500 Diye"*

---
**Tips:**
*   Always mention the amount clearly.
*   Use names with capital letters if possible (e.g., "Ashfaq" instead of "ashfaq").
*   Keep sentences short and direct.
